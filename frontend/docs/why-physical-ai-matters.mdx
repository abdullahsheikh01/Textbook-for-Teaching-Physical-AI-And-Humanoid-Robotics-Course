# Why Physical AI Matters

Physical AI is the integration of artificial intelligence with physical systems, enabling robots and other embodied agents to perceive, reason, and act in the real world. This textbook explores the fundamental concepts and practical applications of physical AI, covering topics such as:

- **[Robotic Operating System (ROS 2)](/docs/modules/module-1)**: The nervous system of modern robots.
- **[Digital Twins (Gazebo & Unity)](/docs/modules/module-2)**: Simulating complex robotic environments.
- **[AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac™)](/docs/modules/module-3)**: Advanced AI frameworks for robotics.
- **[Vision-Language-Action (VLA)](/docs/modules/module-4)**: Enabling robots to understand and interact with their surroundings.

## Course Structure

The course is organized into four comprehensive modules, each building upon the previous to provide a complete understanding of Physical AI:

### [Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)](/docs/modules/module-1)
Learn the fundamentals of ROS 2, the middleware that enables communication between different components of robotic systems.

### [Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)](/docs/modules/module-2)
Explore simulation environments that allow for safe testing and development of robotic systems before deployment.

### [Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac™)](/docs/modules/module-3)
Discover advanced AI frameworks that provide perception, navigation, and manipulation capabilities.

### [Module 4: Vision-Language-Action (VLA)](/docs/modules/module-4)
Understand the cutting-edge integration of vision, language, and action for intuitive human-robot interaction.

## Learning Path

We recommend following the course in sequence, with each module containing:
- [Weekly breakdowns](/docs/weekly-breakdowns/week-1) for structured learning
- [Assessments](/docs/assessments/module-1-assessment) to test your understanding
- Practical exercises to reinforce concepts

Through this course, you will gain a comprehensive understanding of how AI is transforming the field of robotics and learn to build intelligent systems that can operate autonomously in diverse physical environments.
